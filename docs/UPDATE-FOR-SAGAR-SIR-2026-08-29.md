## 1. Pichhle 5 din mein kya-kya complete ho chuka hai

Ye sab kaam ho chuka hai aur test bhi ho chuka hai.

**✓ Clients ki private jaankari ab surakshit hai**
Pehle har team member poori client list dekh sakta tha. Ab sirf Master aur Admin hi dekh sakte
hain — baaki kisi ko nahi dikhti.

**✓ Salary ki jaankari ab poori team ki dikhti hai**
Pehle sirf ek hi member dikhta tha. Ab poori team dikhti hai, aur jinki details adhoori hain unke
saamne saaf likha aata hai ki jaankari baaki hai. Bank details sirf Master ko dikhti hain.

**✓ Claim ke saath document lagana ab possible hai**
Staff ab photo kheench sakte hain, gallery se image laga sakte hain, file laga sakte hain, aur video
bhi record kar sakte hain. App khud video ko chhota kar deti hai taaki wo aasani se chala jaaye.

**✓ Report banna theek ho gaya**
Pehle report bante-bante 12 second mein hi ruk jaati thi aur fail ho jaati thi. Ab use poora time
milta hai, isliye report ban jaati hai.

**✓ Task aur calendar dono behtar hue**
Tasks mein search aa gaya hai jo spelling galat hone par bhi dhoondh leta hai, poore mahine ka
calendar aa gaya hai, aur ab har team member apna khud ka task bana sakta hai.

**✓ Location ab har ghante record hoti hai**
Aapke kehne par ise har minute se badalkar har ghanta kar diya gaya hai. Isse staff ke phone ki
battery aur mobile data dono kaafi bachte hain.

**✓ Location ka hisaab ab sahi lagta hai**
Pehle staff ke duty shuru karne se pehle ka ghoomna-firna bhi duty ke samay mein jud jaata tha. Ab
wo alag rakha jaata hai, isliye duty ka record sahi banta hai.

**✓ App ab achanak band nahi hoti**
Pehle agar kahin koi dikkat aa jaati thi to poori app band ho jaati thi aur user ko kuch samajh nahi
aata tha. Ab ek safety net laga diya hai — user ko saaf message dikhta hai aur app sambhal jaati hai.

**✓ Login ke message insaani bhasha mein aa gaye**
Pehle login fail hone par screen par ajeeb se computer wale shabd dikhte the. Ab saaf-saaf likha
aata hai ki kya galat hua aur kya karna hai.

**✓ Suraksha ki chaar baar poori jaanch hui**
Poori app ko chaar baar detail mein check kiya gaya. Lagbhag 18 chhoti-badi kamiyan pakdi aur theek
ki gayin — jaise ek hi phone par do log login karein to pehle wale ka data reh jaana.

## 2. Hum abhi kis cheez par kaam kar rahe hain

In teen cheezon par abhi kaam chal raha hai.

### Voice Assistant

Staff app se bolkar apna kaam kar sakenge — jaise *"aaj mere kitne task hain"* ya *"meri attendance
batao"* — aur app unhe bolkar jawab degi. Hindi, Gujarati aur English, teeno mein.

App ke andar ka poora hissa ban chuka hai — bolne ka button, sunne wali screen, jawab dene ka
tarika, aur suraksha ke niyam (kaun kya pooch sakta hai) — sab taiyaar hai.

**Pending:** Server par ek chhota hissa banna baaki hai, jo aawaz ko shabdon mein aur shabdon ko
wapas aawaz mein badalta hai. Uske baad phone par test karna hai.

### Translation — shabd aur section

App ko paanch bhashaon mein laa rahe hain, taaki har staff member apni bhasha mein kaam kar sake.
Ye kaam thoda-thoda karke aage badh raha hai — kuch shabd, kuch poore section.

**Ab tak 446 shabd aur vaakya translate ho chuke hain.** Sign-in screen, More menu, home screen ke
kai hisse, clock-in aur clock-out ka poora flow, aur status ke saare shabd translate ho chuke hain.

**Pending:** Kuch section abhi English mein hain. Unke liye humein aapki taraf se shabd chahiye —
kyunki machine se translate karna theek nahi rehta, galat matlab nikal aata hai.

### Store Deployment — App Store aur Play Store

App ko Apple ke App Store aur Google ke Play Store, dono par daalne ki taiyari chal rahi hai. App ki
taraf ka kaam lagbhag 90% poora ho chuka hai — jo kagzi kaam aur account chahiye, wo bacha hai.

**Pending:** Iski poori list neeche "hum kahan atke hain" wale hisse mein di gayi hai — kyunki yahi
wo jagah hai jahan sabse zyada cheezein ruki hui hain.

## 2. App Store aur Play Store — poora breakdown

App ko dono stores par daalne ke liye kuch cheezein chahiye jo sirf company hi kar sakti hai. Ek-ek
karke saral bhasha mein:

### (A) Apple Developer Account

- Ye account **kharidna padega** — iski keemat **$99 hai, aur ye har saal deni padti hai.**
- Iski process thodi lambi hai — Apple company ke documents check karta hai, verify karta hai. Isme
  kuch din se lekar kuch hafte tak lag sakte hain.
- Isliye ise **sabse pehle shuru karna behtar rahega**, kyunki baaki sab iske baad hi ho sakta hai.

> ⚠️ Iske bina iPhone par app bilkul nahi jaa sakti — koi doosra rasta nahi hai.

### (B) Google Play Account

- Iska **naya account banana padega.**
- Iski fees **lagbhag $25 se $30 hai** — aur achhi baat ye hai ki ye **sirf ek hi baar deni hoti
  hai**, Apple ki tarah har saal nahi.
- Account banne ke baad **app review ke liye deni padegi.** Google ki team khud app ko kholkar check
  karti hai ki sab theek hai ya nahi. Isme kuch din lagte hain.

### (C) Do nayi website pages banani hain

- **Privacy page** — ek page jismein saaf likha ho ki app kaun si jaankari leti hai aur kyun leti hai.
- **Account delete page** — ek page jahan se koi bhi apna account hamesha ke liye hata sake.
- Dono pages company ki website par **live honi chahiye.**

> ⚠️ Ye dono stores ki pakki shart hai — inke bina app reject ho jaati hai.

### (D) Store ke liye tasveerein aur jaankari

- App ke andar ki **screenshots** — jisse log dekh sakein ki app kaisi dikhti hai.
- Ek **bada banner picture** jo store par sabse upar dikhta hai.
- **App ka icon** saaf aur badi size mein.
- App ka **chhota sa parichay** — ki ye app kya karti hai.

> ⚠️ Ye store par app ki dukaan ki window hai — iske bina app list hi nahi ho sakti.

### (E) Review ke liye ek demo account

- Apple aur Google, dono ki team khud app kholkar check karti hai.
- Unhe hume **ek login (ID aur password) dena padta hai**, jisse wo poori app ghoom kar dekh sakein.
- Ye account hume banakar dena hoga, aur usme thoda saara sample data bhi hona chahiye.

> ⚠️ Login diye bina store waale app ko reject kar dete hain.
